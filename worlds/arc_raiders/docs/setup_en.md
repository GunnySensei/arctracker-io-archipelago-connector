# ARC Raiders: Archipelago Setup

1. Make an arctracker.io account and link your ARC Raiders account to it (ARC Tracker Link app).
2. In arctracker.io, open Settings → Developer Access and create a read-only user key.
3. Put `arc_raiders.apworld` in your Archipelago `custom_worlds` folder.
4. Open the Archipelago Launcher, choose **ARC Raiders Client**, and paste your keys the first time it asks.
5. Connect to the room. The client records where your account is right now, and only raids after that point count.

Items you receive set what you may bring into a raid. You follow them on your honor; the client warns you if your synced loadout breaks a rule.
