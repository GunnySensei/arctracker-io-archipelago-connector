Archipelago world · v0.4.0

ARC Raiders × Archipelago

Checks come from real raids: extract, kill ARC, loot cores, finish quests. They're detected automatically through arctracker.io. Items you receive set what you may bring topside, from grey pistols and two bandages up to gold guns and heavy shields.

Works on your existing account: only progress after you first connect counts. Nothing touches the game itself. The client only reads your synced raids from arctracker.io.

1 · Install

You need ARC Raiders (Steam or Epic) linked to a free arctracker.io account through the ARC Tracker Link app, plus Archipelago 0.6.4 or newer (tested on 0.6.7) on Windows.

  1. Download the latest release from the Download page, unzip it, and double-click arc_raiders.apworld. The Archipelago Launcher installs it. (Or copy it into Archipelago\custom_worlds\.)
  2. Restart the Launcher. ARC Raiders Client and ARC Raiders HUD now appear in its list.

2 · Your arctracker.io keys

One-time setup, about three minutes. Both keys stay on your PC.

  1. On arctracker.io: Settings → Developer Access → create a key with the read scopes (profile, rounds, stash, loadout, quests, blueprints, hideout, projects). It starts with arc_u1_. This is your user key.
  2. Developer Dashboard → register an app (any name). Its app key starts with arc_k1_. Make your own app key rather than sharing one: each app key has a 500 calls/hour limit.
  3. You'll paste both into the client the first time you connect.

3 · Your settings (YAML)

Use the YAML generator: pick your settings, download the file and send it to the host. (Or copy ARC Raiders.yaml from the release and edit it by hand.) The defaults make a good first run: the Core Hunt goal (loot 10 different ARC cores, one a Matriarch or Queen Reactor), about 105 checks, and 20% of fillers as traps.

4 · Hosting (one person)

  1. Put everyone's YAMLs in Archipelago\Players\ and run Generate. The host needs this apworld installed, plus apworlds for any other custom games in the multiworld.
  2. Upload the .zip from Archipelago\output\ at archipelago.gg/uploads, or host it locally.
  3. Only ARC Raiders players need the apworld on their own PCs.

5 · Playing

  1. Sync arctracker.io first (their website's sync button), so your latest raids are already in.
  2. Open ARC Raiders Client, enter the server address and your slot name, and connect.
  3. First time only: in the ARC Raiders tab, click Keys, paste both keys, and click Save keys.
  4. The client takes a starting snapshot. Nothing before this moment counts.
  5. Click Desktop HUD, then raid as normal. After raids, sync arctracker.io (subscribers sync automatically). The client checks every 2 minutes, or press CHECK.

  • A transparent panel on top of the game. The game must be in borderless windowed mode.
  • Pop-up cards for every check you hit (teal), every item someone sends you (amber), and traps (red). Up to 4 show at once; extras wait their turn.
  • Move it by dragging the dotted triangle in its top-right corner. Right-click to change the view (Full / Compact / Pop-ups only), scale, opacity or position, or to close it.
  • DONE clears a trap, CHECK looks for new raids now, and SYNC opens arctracker.io (this tabs you out).
  • It never takes focus, so clicks and new cards won't release your mouse from the game.

The rules: what you may bring

You start under-geared; items raise these tiers. A gun is allowed only if its rarity, tier and class are all unlocked. The referee in the client flags your last synced loadout if it breaks a rule.

ItemStart → max
Weapon RarityGrey → Green → Blue → Purple → Gold
Weapon TierI only → up to II → up to III → up to IV (gold/legendary guns count as IV)
ShieldsNone → Light → Medium → Heavy
Healing2 grey heals → 4 (≤ green) → 6 (≤ blue) → 10 (any) → unlimited
ThrowablesNone → 2 → 5 → unlimited (grenades and mines)
AugmentFree/basic → ≤ green → ≤ blue → any
Weapon Slots1 gun → 2 guns
Weapon LicenseOnly licensed classes (AR, BR, SMG, Shotgun, Pistol, Hand Cannon, Sniper, LMG, Special). You start with 2.
Map AccessOnly unlocked maps. You start with 1 of 7.

Checks & goals

  • Extraction #N on any map, and <Map> Extraction #N (needs that map unlocked)
  • Extract a Raid Worth 10k / 25k / 50k / 100k / 150k / 200k+
  • Total Extracted Milestone #N: one for every 50k of value you extract
  • ARC Kills: 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 350 / 500
  • Loot a <Core> #1 / #2: 19 ARC cores, from Tick Pods and Wasp Drivers through Rocketeer Drivers and Bastion Cells to the Matriarch and Queen Reactors. A core counts when it shows up in your stash or backpack.
  • New Quest Completed, New Blueprint Learned, Hideout Upgrade, Project Phase Completed #N

Goals: core_hunt (loot N different cores, optionally including a boss reactor), extracted_value (default 500k) or both. Harder checks expect more kit; boss cores expect blue guns, tier III, Medium shields and 6 heals.

Client commands: /arc_status /arc_poll /arc_spend /arc_done /arc_undo /arc_hud /arc_overlay /arc_voice on|off

Troubleshooting

You seeDo this
Red “Sync needed”Your last arctracker sync is older than your snapshot. Sync on the website. If a sync carries old raids, their cores are skipped and the client tells you why.
Nothing after a raidThe raid isn't on arctracker yet. Sync on the website, then press CHECK.
HUD not visible in gameSwitch the game to borderless windowed. Exclusive fullscreen draws over it.
Keys error (401/403)Re-create the user key with all read scopes and check the app key.
API calls running lowThe client slows down by itself. Use your own app key, not a shared one.
